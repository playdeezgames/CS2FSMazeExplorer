**Maze Explorer of SPLORR!!**

A small, strict maze crawl against the clock. Find your way through a maze you can only see a little of at a time, open locks, fight what lives there, pick up what you can carry, and reach the exit before the time runs out.

**CONTROLS**

Arrow keys move and turn. Space pauses, Q quits to the title, Esc goes back, F1 is help, F2 starts a new game, F3 is options, M mutes, P toggles whether pausing is allowed, F goes fullscreen. On a phone, on-screen buttons appear. Your sound setting, difficulty and best scores are remembered in this browser.

**ABOUT**

This is a port of Maze Explorer, a sample game written in F# for the "C# to F#" blog series (csharptofsharp.wordpress.com), rebuilt in Odin and WebAssembly with the original rules, tables, art and font kept as they were, quirks included. Add `?seed=N` to the address to replay a particular maze.
